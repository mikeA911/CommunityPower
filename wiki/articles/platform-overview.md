# About Community Power and CP

Draft describing the planned platform. No live service is available from this repository yet.

Community Power is intended to help consumers understand bills, coordinate voluntarily with their communities, compare reviewed service offers and track outcomes. It is supplier-neutral. Joining a community does not establish electricity-service eligibility or guarantee savings.

CP is the planned AI assistant. You can ask him for explanations and help with tasks; important actions use review and confirmation cards. Direct controls and manual entry remain available when AI is unavailable. CP cannot sign a contract, switch service or consent to sharing on your behalf.

The planned free tier includes a basic bill overview, basic comparisons and the community board. Optional upgrades are separate. The board is a shared community space; your private CP conversation is separate.

Sandz's platform admins provide support and maintenance. Their role does not give unrestricted access to consumer bills. Account-specific information requires the appropriate authority, and sensitive support access is scoped and audited.

## Example question

“Will Community Power definitely save me money?”

Answer points: savings are not guaranteed; comparisons depend on verified inputs and complete offer terms; negative outcomes must be shown; estimates and measured savings are different.
