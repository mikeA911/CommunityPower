# Long-term product vision
Preserved themes from the artifact inventory: community services, payments, EV, parking, solar, smart infrastructure and other services. The sequencing below is proposed.

Phase 1: demonstrate lawful energy coordination, comprehensible offers, trusted communications and measurable member outcomes. Build a repeatable onboarding and support process before expanding geography or services.

Phase 2: deepen energy capabilities through richer reporting, supplier workflows, solar feasibility referrals and appropriately validated efficiency services. Require actual demand, positive incremental contribution and reliable partners before launch. Renewable claims need their own evidence and accounting method.

Phase 3: test adjacent community services such as EV charging coordination and parking services where the community has authority and a clear user need. Evaluate each as a separate product, with its own operational costs and contracts. An energy user base does not prove demand for every adjacent service.

Phase 4: consider payments and smart infrastructure only after assessing additional regulation, custody, hardware, support and security responsibilities. No wallet, payment custody or infrastructure asset purchase is included in the MVP envelope.

Expansion gate: validated member problem; appropriate authority; viable partner/service delivery; incremental economics; privacy review; support capacity; no erosion of supplier-neutral energy comparisons. Stop or sequence down initiatives that distract from evidence of core value.
