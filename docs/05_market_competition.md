Status: research framework with limited verified historical background, not a current market survey.

## Policy context
The ERC announced Omnibus Rules for consumer choice programs in August 2024, covering RCOA, retail aggregation and other programs. This establishes relevant policy background, not Community Power’s eligibility. [ERC announcement](https://www.erc.gov.ph/Press-Singular/83808). The ERC also records a 2022 retail aggregation resolution. [Issuance record](https://erc.gov.ph/Issuance-Detail/41748). Subsequent amendments, effective dates, registration processes and route-specific requirements must be checked against current authoritative texts.

The commercial hypothesis is that fragmented customer effort and hard-to-compare offers leave room for an independent community coordination service. Validate it with interviews and actual proposals; do not assume regulation creates a serviceable market of all Philippine households. Establish the reachable market bottom-up from eligible accounts, permission to serve, viable suppliers, and obtainable communities.

## Comparable alternatives to test
Members may stay with existing arrangements, contact suppliers directly, use a broker or procurement adviser, or rely on association management. Community Power must show incremental value over these alternatives after its own cost and administrative effort. A no-switch recommendation is a valid product outcome.

Compare: eligible customer segment; direct supply versus advice/software role; community aggregation support; disclosure of compensation; number and coverage of offers; onboarding effort; bill analytics; measured savings methodology; community governance/message board; optional upgrade pricing; integration and support costs. Mark every unanswered cell unknown rather than inferring absence.

## Research protocol and deliverable
Commercial lead first resolves identities using official company materials and registry/regulator records. Then request comparable written offers or demos, record the date and customer/account scope, and interview community sponsors and residents. Capture evidence links and exact quoted fee conditions in a comparison table. Review with counsel when descriptions imply regulated activity. Supplier neutrality, community engagement, and transparent measured benefits are differentiation hypotheses until validated.

No quantified TAM/SAM/SOM is asserted. Build an addressable-account list first; multiplying every household by a subscription price would overstate the presently evidenced opportunity.
