## Evidence record template
ID; status (open/in progress/validated/blocked); named owner; exact question; source/link and relevant section; publication/effective/access dates; applicable community/account scope; finding; reviewer; decision; expiry/review trigger. A checked source or signed commercial term does not close unrelated questions.

## Priority order
Resolve V01–V04 before committing to a commercial route; assess V05–V08 alongside early community interviews; resolve V09–V13 before setting financial expectations and launching; complete V14 before publishing named comparisons. Changes in rules, contracts, account configuration, data processing or pricing reopen the affected review.
